I.

Glitched marble

Borrowed from the Hermes site

The electric blue field. Oswald Light for headlines, the same open-license face Hermes uses. Small tracked capitals for labels. Classical art screened into blue.

Ours alone

A pixel hand with one slice knocked sideways. One signal yellow, kept for what is not yet tested. Two plates cut from public-domain marbles: a head that stays put, and a hand that is somewhere else.

Kept clear of

The Hermes wing, the Nous marks, and the word "official". Every page says community plugin. The catalog reserves the official tier for Nous, so the page should never hint at it.
